Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Evenly is a free app for sharing expenses with friends. This page explains what information it keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

What Evenly keeps

  • Your account: your name, email address and, if you add one, a profile photo. If you sign in with Google, Evenly receives your name, email address and Google profile photo, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you add: groups, friends, expenses, payments, comments, receipt photos and notes.
  • Optional settings: your default currency, monthly budgets, payment details such as a UPI ID (shown only to people you share expenses with), and notification preferences.
  • Phone notifications: if you turn them on, a technical address for your device so Evenly can deliver them.

Who can see it

People you share a group or expense with can see your name, photo, and the expenses and comments in that group. People can find you by name when adding friends; they see a hint such as j•••@gmail.com, never your full email address. Nobody else can see your data.

How it’s used

Only to run Evenly: working out balances, showing your activity, and sending the notifications you’ve chosen. Evenly has no ads, does not sell or share your information, and does not use it for any other purpose.

Where it’s stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, which hosts Evenly’s database, and the app is served by Netlify. Exchange rates are fetched from a public rates service without sending any of your information. A copy of your balances is kept on your device so the app works offline.

Your choices

  • Change your name, photo and settings any time in Account.
  • Download everything you can see as a file from Account → Your data.
  • Delete your account from Account → Delete account. Your login and profile are removed; expenses you shared stay with the group under your name so their balances still add up.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
